← Back to Rally

Bring your original notebook along.

The original Rally saves notes in your browser. It has no backup button, but this small bookmark can download them. Ask a parent to help on a desktop browser.

  1. Show your browser’s bookmarks bar. Drag this link onto it:
    Export Rally notebook

  2. Open the original Rally in the same browser and profile where you saved your notes.
  3. While on the original Rally, click your new Export Rally notebook bookmark. A JSON backup downloads.
  4. Return to this Rally, open My notebook → Restore backup, and choose that file.

Your old notes stay where they are. The bookmark reads only Rally’s notebook, downloads it to your computer, and sends nothing anywhere.

What does the bookmark do?

It checks that you’re on the original Rally address, reads the rally-notebook entry in browser storage, and downloads it as rally-original-notebook.json. If browser policy blocks bookmark scripts, your notes remain available at the original site.
